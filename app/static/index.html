About THERMOGRAPH Prototype (Gujarat)

Core Concept

"THERMOGRAPH classifies places, not individual hotspots."

NASA FIRMS active fire satellites detect thermal signatures, but individual hotspots alone do not indicate whether an observation represents routine flaring, agricultural clearing, or an unusual industrial event. THERMOGRAPH connects thermal observations to geography, historical site continuity, and facility context.

Methodology & Workflow

  • Data Monitoring: 12 months of NASA FIRMS (VIIRS) satellite observations (1 Jul 2025 – 30 Jun 2026, 21,513 detections) covering Gujarat, India.
  • Spatial Clustering: Repeated thermal detections are grouped into persistent candidate thermal sites using DBSCAN (haversine metric, 1.0 km radius, minimum 5 detections). Unassigned detections are preserved as isolated hotspots.
  • Facility Context: Sites are matched against 710 OpenStreetMap (OSM) industrial features in projected coordinates (EPSG:32643, UTM zone 43N). Matches are categorized as inside polygon boundary, nearby (≤1,000 m), or unmapped.
  • Historical Baselines: Weeks 0–38 (1 Jul 2025 – 30 Mar 2026, 39 weeks) establish normal behavior. Sites active in ≥10 baseline weeks establish a statistical baseline with upper limit threshold = mean + 3×std dev of weekly heat output (FRP sum).
  • Transparent Anomaly Logic: Monitoring weeks 39–51 (31 Mar 2026 – 30 Jun 2026) detect statistical baseline exceedances and new activity. Priority levels (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) are scored using documented, transparent prototype points.

Scientific Limitations & Cautions (PRD §11)

  • Observational Indicators Only: Results are observational indicators requiring ground verification. They are not confirmed emergency incidents, environmental violations, or real-world alerts.
  • 12-Month Single-Year Baseline: 12 months of observations cannot separate recurring seasonal patterns (e.g. crop-residue burning or monsoon cloud attenuation) from true industrial anomalies.
  • Cloud Cover Attenuation: Satellites cannot detect thermal radiation through dense cloud cover; monsoon weeks may undercount activity and depress historical averages.
  • OSM Incompleteness: OpenStreetMap data is community-sourced. "Unmapped" indicates no mapped industrial feature within 1 km in OSM, never that a source is illegal or non-industrial.
  • No Machine Learning: No predictive AI or synthetic "confidence %" is used. All classifications are transparent rule-based prototype settings.
